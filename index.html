Drop in an event order (BEO, PDF) and/or a statement of charges (SOC, Word). The tool reads the charges, checks every per-person and package price against the 2026 menu price book, and — when you load both — confirms the BEO and SOC agree. Files are read in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

View the 2026 menu price book this checks against

How the price check works. Each charge line is matched by name to the menu price book (tier-aware, so a “6 Hour” package only matches the 6-hour price). Matched lines are compared penny-for-penny: correct, undercharged or overcharged, with the per-person difference and the total impact across the guest count. The tool also recomputes qty × price to catch arithmetic slips. Operational charges with no menu price — audio-visual, security, room hire, attrition / minimum spend — are listed as non-menu for you to confirm manually.

Prices come from the E&C Menu 2026 price book. Food and most beverages are valid 01 Jul 2026 – 30 Jun 2027; the Christmas buffet to 31 Dec 2026. If a menu changes, update src/prices.js and reload.
